Go2ShellNext

Open terminal from Finder

⌘

Add to Finder Toolbar

Hold ⌘ and drag Go2ShellNext.app onto the Finder toolbar

Executed after cd-ing to the target directory

Usage

  • Click the Go2ShellNext icon in Finder toolbar → terminal opens at current folder
  • Open Go2ShellNext.app from Spotlight → shows this settings window
  • To remove from toolbar: hold ⌘ and drag the icon off
